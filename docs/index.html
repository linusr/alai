Alai Notify

Your notifications, your server

Alai is a native iPhone and Apple Watch app for a notification server that you run yourself. Scripts, services and devices publish to your Alai server, and Alai delivers the messages to your iPhone, Apple Watch, widgets and Spotlight. It also works with other ntfy-compatible servers, without instant push.

  • Instant push through Apple Push Notification service, sent directly by your own Alai server
  • Topics list, an hour-by-hour calendar of the day's notifications, and search across every message
  • Markdown, priorities, tags, attachments and action buttons
  • Home and Lock Screen widgets, and an Apple Watch app with complications
  • No accounts with us, no analytics, no tracking

Alai is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ntfy project.
